About Sentiment Analysis

Enterprise-grade 3-class sentiment intelligence platform

🧠 What is Sentiment Analysis?

Sentiment Analysis is an advanced NLP sentiment intelligence platform that classifies natural language text as Positive, Negative, or Neutral. Built with state-of-the-art NLP pipelines, robust feature extraction, and multiple model architectures, it delivers high-confidence predictions on reviews, comments, and social media posts.

📊 Dataset
  • Source: Yelp Reviews Full / Amazon Reviews (5-star → 3-class mapping)
  • Training: 45,000 samples (perfectly balanced, 15k per class)
  • Testing: 7,500 samples (held-out, 2.5k per class)
  • Classes: Negative (1★), Neutral (3★), Positive (5★)
  • Supported Domains: Movie Reviews (IMDb), Product Reviews (Amazon), Business Reviews (Yelp)

⚠️ Known Limitations
  • Neutral class is inherently challenging — texts without strong opinion words often fall between categories
  • Sarcasm and irony may be misclassified (e.g., "Oh great, another delay!")
  • Domain-specific vocabulary (medical, legal, technical) may reduce accuracy
  • Non-English text is not supported
  • Very short texts (1–3 words) may have lower confidence predictions
  • Confidence scores from probabilistic models are not perfectly calibrated
