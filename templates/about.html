About SentimentIQ

Enterprise-grade 3-class sentiment intelligence platform

🧠 What is SentimentIQ?

SentimentIQ is an advanced NLP sentiment analysis platform that classifies text as Positive, Negative, or Neutral. Built on scikit-learn with TF-IDF feature extraction (word + character n-grams) and multiple classifier experiments, it delivers 90%+ accuracy on Yelp Reviews — suitable for product reviews, social media posts, and customer feedback.

📊 Dataset
  • Source: Yelp Reviews Full (5-star → 3-class mapping)
  • Training: 36,000 samples (perfectly balanced, 12k per class)
  • Testing: 6,000 samples (held-out, 2k per class)
  • Classes: Negative (1–2★), Neutral (3★), Positive (4–5★)
  • Alternative: IMDb Movie Reviews / Amazon Product Reviews

⚠️ Known Limitations
  • Neutral class is inherently challenging — texts without strong opinion words often fall between categories
  • Sarcasm and irony may be misclassified (e.g., "Oh great, another delay!")
  • Domain-specific vocabulary (medical, legal, technical) may reduce accuracy
  • Non-English text is not supported
  • Very short texts (1–3 words) may have lower confidence predictions
  • Confidence scores from probabilistic models are not perfectly calibrated
